## Mandatory qualifications (apply to every number above)

1. The interval captures **subject-level sampling only** -- the three training seeds are averaged,
   not resampled, so training-seed variability is **not** inside the interval and the interval is
   correspondingly narrow. It must never be read as a total-uncertainty interval.
2. **No pairwise comparison here may be promised as a positive effect.** Directional consistency
   across sites and seeds is reported as observed heterogeneity, not as an established gain.
3. The GPU-time figure for this campaign is an **estimate** derived from observed per-fit wall time,
   not a measured total.
4. Fold AUCs on 6-8-subject test folds are extremely noisy by construction; cell-level and
   seed-level values are recorded for traceability and are not interpreted individually.
5. Integrity: every fold AUC reported here was recomputed from the saved OOF logits and matched the
   run report exactly (mismatches: 0). Degenerate folds: 0.
6. This experiment is a **new, separate** analysis (seeds {666, 2025, 2026}); it does not reuse or
   overwrite the historical seed set, and it changes no earlier reported number by itself.
7. No p value outside this document's own family enters any Holm calculation until the 13-item
   family in `STATISTICAL_FAMILY_13.md` is closed (currently 7/13 have a locatable raw p).
   For Major 1's OWN 18-site family the raw p values are additionally Holm-adjusted within that
   family (`p_holm_18family` in `major1_site_level.csv`); the two families are never pooled.
